Welcome to Vibesomnia

You can sleep. Your AI doesn’t have to.

Install Vibesomnia in Applications to keep your Mac awake for AI apps and remote work.

On first launch, setup helps you find your apps, choose sleep preferences, and test your existing remote connection with the lid closed.

Before updating: end your session and quit Vibesomnia. This installer does not enable remote access, change sleep settings, or install a background service.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Beta software: physical lid and remote compatibility are still being tested.
